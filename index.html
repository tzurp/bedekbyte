02 THE PERSON BEHIND BEDEKBYTE

Who are we?

My name is Tzur Paldi, and Bedekbyte is built on decades of curiosity, learning, and hands-on engineering. I wrote my first programs as a kid, experimenting with whatever computer I could get my hands on. Years later, after completing my PhD, software development became my profession and eventually my craft.

I’ve spent my career exploring not only how to write code, but how to build reliable systems, effective development processes, and engineering cultures that scale. I’ve led projects and development efforts in major hi-tech companies, including Microsoft, where precision and quality are part of the DNA.

Bedekbyte is the continuation of that journey: a collection of tools, ideas, and practices designed to help developers build better software with confidence.
